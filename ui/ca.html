Cost of Borrowing Calculator

Calculate the cost of borrowing for a mortgage or personal loan over its current term: the COB amount and rate, total interest, and a full payment schedule. For variable-rate mortgages it also shows the trigger rate. Fixed-rate mortgage rates are compounded semi-annually.

Note: results are estimates. They don't replace the cost of borrowing disclosure in the loan documents.
